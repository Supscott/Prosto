uBlock Origin redirect resources

`app/src/main/assets/adblock/resources.json` contains the contents of the files in
`src/web_accessible_resources/` of uBlock Origin 1.76.2 (see UBO_TAG in
tools/build_adblock_resources.py), base64-encoded, with the resource names and aliases from
`src/js/redirect-resources.js`.
